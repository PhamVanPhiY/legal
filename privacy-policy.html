Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, games, utilities, and related digital services (collectively, the "Applications" or "Services") developed, published, or maintained by DoubleP Tech ("we", "us", or "our").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is handled. By installing, accessing, or using any of our Applications, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Applications.

1. Information We Collect

We design our Applications to minimize the collection of personal information. We do not require users to create an account or provide personally identifiable information (such as your legal name, home address, or phone number) to enjoy our core features.

However, to provide reliable functionality, improve performance, and deliver personalized experiences, our Applications and integrated third-party SDKs may collect certain technical and usage data:

  • Device & Technical Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, system language, screen resolution, connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular), and anonymous device identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID).
  • Usage Analytics & Telemetry: Anonymized interaction metrics (such as features accessed, levels completed, tool selections, session durations, and launch frequency) used to improve application design and user satisfaction.
  • Crash Reporting & Diagnostics: Diagnostic logs, stack traces, and system state at the time of an unexpected error, helping us quickly identify and resolve technical glitches.
  • In-App Purchase & Subscription Status: When you purchase virtual items, ad-free access, or subscriptions, transaction identifiers and purchase tokens generated by Google Play Billing are processed to unlock your entitlements. We never receive, collect, or store your credit card or financial banking details.

2. Feature-Specific Device Permissions

Depending on the specific DoubleP Tech Application you install, certain features may request runtime Android permissions. These permissions are used exclusively to enable requested functionality on your device:

  • Storage / Photos / Media: Requested only when you explicitly choose to export, save, or share created images, artwork, or files to your device storage.
  • Bluetooth & Local Network: Requested by utility applications (such as remote control, mouse, or presenter utilities) to discover and communicate with paired devices.
  • Microphone / Audio: Requested only by apps featuring sound recording, audio effects, or interactive music visualizers. Audio is processed on-device and never transmitted to external servers.
  • Notifications: Used only to deliver relevant app reminders, new content alerts, or level unlocks if you grant permission.

You can grant or revoke any optional permission at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Permissions).

In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing. Third-party billing platforms (such as Qonversion) verify purchase tokens to grant premium features (such as removing ads or unlocking VIP content). We never collect or store your payment card details.

4. Advertising and Personalized Ads

Free versions of our Applications may display third-party advertisements to support ongoing development. Ad networks may use device identifiers (GAID) to display ads tailored to your general interests or measure ad performance.

GDPR / Consent Management: For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we implement a Google-certified User Messaging Platform (UMP / CMP) consent dialog that allows you to accept or customize your consent for personalized versus non-personalized advertising.

Opting Out: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset / Delete advertising ID.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Our Applications are designed for general audiences and family-friendly entertainment. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information without verifiable parental consent, we promptly delete such data. Parents or guardians may contact us directly with any inquiries.

6. Data Retention and Security

We do not maintain user databases containing personal data. Telemetry and crash reports processed by third-party SDKs are retained in accordance with standard industry retention schedules (e.g., Google Cloud, Firebase). We utilize industry-standard TLS encryption for all network communications.

  • The right to access and know what data is collected.
  • The right to request deletion of data.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data for personalized advertising.
