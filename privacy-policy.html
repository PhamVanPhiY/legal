Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DoubleP Tech ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you download, install, and play Color Page ASMR: Art Coloring (the "Application" or "App").

By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not install or use the Application.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy. The Application is designed as a relaxing coloring book experience. We do not require users to create an account or provide personal identifiers like full legal names, physical addresses, or phone numbers to use the App.

However, the Application utilizes trusted third-party services that automatically collect standard device and usage information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID - GAID), language settings, and network connection type.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous game interaction data (such as levels completed, drawing durations, tool preferences, and app launch timestamps) to help us improve performance and design.
  • Crash & Performance Logs: Diagnostic data and stack traces generated when unexpected errors occur, used to identify and fix bugs.
  • In-App Purchase Receipts: When you purchase subscriptions or in-app items, transaction identifiers and purchase tokens generated by Google Play are processed to unlock premium content. We never receive or store your credit card or banking credentials.

In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

All in-app financial transactions are handled securely by Google Play Billing. When you subscribe to our VIP plans (Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly), Google processes your billing. Qonversion verifies the Google Play purchase token to grant your VIP access (such as removing all ads and unlocking all artworks). We do not collect or store your payment details.

3. Advertising and Personalized Ads

We display third-party advertisements via Google AdMob to keep core gameplay free. AdMob may use device identifiers (Advertising ID) and device characteristics to display relevant advertisements or measure ad performance.

In regions governed by the GDPR (European Economic Area / UK), we present a Google-certified User Messaging Platform (UMP / CMP) consent dialog allowing you to manage your consent preferences for personalized vs. non-personalized advertising.

You can also reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete or Reset Advertising ID.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Application is designed for general audiences and creative relaxation. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, we take immediate steps to delete such data from our servers. Parents or guardians with concerns may contact us at the email provided below.

5. Data Retention and Security

We do not operate proprietary user account databases containing personal information. Telemetry, analytics, and crash logs collected by third-party SDKs are retained in accordance with the standard retention schedules of Google Cloud, Firebase, and Qonversion.

We implement industry-standard technical measures (such as TLS encryption) to protect data transmitted between your device and our third-party infrastructure.

To exercise your rights, please submit a request to our contact email address below.
